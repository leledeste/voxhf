Optional remote alert

Notify this device if VoxHF goes offline?

During an active IVAO session, the relay can alert this device if the local proxy, simulator PC, or network path stops responding.

Memory onlyThe relay does not save the ticket to its database or backups.
Sealed locallyThe alert is encrypted and signed by your local VoxHF proxy.
Short livedThe ticket and its signature expire within 15 minutes.

The relay receives this device's Push service address and the sealed alert, but not your VAPID private key, chat, audio, position, or flight plan. A modified relay could replay only this same alert until it expires; it cannot read or change it.
